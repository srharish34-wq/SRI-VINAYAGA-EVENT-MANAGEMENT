Who we are

A family-run team behind every stage in the neighbourhood

Sri Vinayagaa Wedding Decoration and Event Management Planner has been building stages, backdrops and full event setups for over a decade — starting with a handful of local weddings and growing into the name families across Chennai now call first.

Balaji and Viswa lead every booking personally, from the first phone call to the last light switched off. We work with Hindu, Christian and Muslim ceremonies alike, and take on everything from an intimate baby shower to a 500-guest reception — always with the same attention to detail.

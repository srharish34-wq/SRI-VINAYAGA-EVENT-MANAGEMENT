Our Story

A passion for celebrations, built around people

Sri Vinayagaa focuses on wedding decoration and complete event management — bringing the same care and attention to every celebration, large or small. Our work spans wedding and reception stages, birthday parties and baby showers, house warming ceremonies and new shop openings, along with school and college functions, meetings and ceremonies.

We also handle lighting and DJ sound as part of a complete event setup, so families and organisations can rely on one team from planning through to the final light switched off. Every event is treated as personal — we listen first, then design around what matters to you.
